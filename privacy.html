Privacy Policy · Last updated June 2026
Sweatbuki does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All data stays entirely on your device.

Data Collection

Sweatbuki does not collect any personal information. We do not have servers that receive your data, and we do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Data Storage

All workout logs, personal records, and settings are stored locally on your device using your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device in your browser's localStorage indefinitely until you choose to delete it. Sweatbuki does not have access to your data and does not store it on any external server. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, all stored information will be permanently deleted.

데이터는 사용자 기기의 브라우저 localStorage에 저장되며, 사용자가 직접 삭제하기 전까지 무기한 보관됩니다. 땀북이는 사용자 데이터에 접근할 수 없으며 외부 서버에 저장하지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하거나 브라우저 데이터를 지우면 저장된 모든 정보가 영구 삭제됩니다.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time through one of the following methods:

  1. Use the "데이터 초기화" (Reset Data) option in the Settings menu within the app.
  2. Clear your browser's site data for this app in your device settings.
  3. Uninstall the app, which will remove all locally stored data.

Since all data is stored only on your device, deletion is immediate and complete. No data remains on any external server.

다음 방법 중 하나로 언제든지 모든 데이터를 삭제할 수 있습니다:

  1. 앱 설정 메뉴의 "데이터 초기화" 옵션 사용
  2. 기기 설정에서 해당 앱의 브라우저 사이트 데이터 삭제
  3. 앱 삭제 (저장된 모든 로컬 데이터가 함께 제거됨)

모든 데이터가 사용자 기기에만 저장되므로 삭제는 즉시 완료되며, 외부 서버에 남는 데이터는 없습니다.

Permissions

Sweatbuki does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device permissions.

Third-Party Services

Sweatbuki does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

Sweatbuki does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
